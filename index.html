How does this work?

Cadence compares 20 measurements from your five enrollment sentences, including key holds, pace, overlapping keys, and word boundaries. If you click away, your sentence pauses; return to the field to continue. Time away is excluded from the comparison.

This is an experimental, single-profile verifier. It can confuse similar typists and reject you when your pace, keyboard, or posture changes. The match threshold is a heuristic, not a calibrated probability. Do not use it as your only authentication method.

Timing samples and your profile name stay in this browser’s local storage. Nothing is sent to a server. Capture only runs inside the typing field. Use Delete profile to remove saved timings.
